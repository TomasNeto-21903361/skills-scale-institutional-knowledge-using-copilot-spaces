# OctoAcme — Project Management Docs

This README provides a short summary of OctoAcme's project management processes and direct links to the process documents stored in the `docs/` folder.

## Summary

OctoAcme follows an iterative, customer-first delivery model with clear ownership across roles such as project managers, product leads, developers, QA, and stakeholders. Work begins with a lightweight initiation phase that validates the business problem, aligns stakeholders, and creates a one-pager with goals, success metrics, timeline, and initial risks. The team then turns the approved initiative into a structured plan with a prioritized backlog, acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, and a milestone/release schedule. Throughout execution, the team uses a regular cadence of standups, demos, and status reviews to keep work visible, identify blockers early, and maintain alignment across teams.

The operating model emphasizes transparent communication, measurable outcomes, and continuous learning. Project managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communications; product managers define priorities and success metrics; developers and QA focus on delivery and validation; and stakeholders provide inputs and approvals at key decision points. The team also follows clear quality practices, including CI checks, test coverage, security scanning, PR review expectations, and post-deployment verification. At the end of each milestone or sprint, teams capture lessons learned in retrospectives and track action items so improvements become part of the next cycle.
